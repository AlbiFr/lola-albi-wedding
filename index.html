Colombia 101: Travel Tips

For our guests traveling from afar, here is some helpful information for your trip.

A Note on Security

A key local saying is "No dar papaya". It means don't make yourself an easy target. Be aware of your surroundings and take care of your personal belongings.

More Info
Getting Around

Public transportation can be inefficient for visitors. We highly recommend using ride-sharing apps like Uber or Cabify for a safe and convenient way to navigate the city.

Cash is King

While cards are widely accepted, some smaller shops and taxis operate on a cash-only basis. It's a good idea to have some Colombian Pesos (COP) on hand for daily expenses.
For a better exchange rate we highly recommend Revolut

Stay Connected

To avoid high roaming charges, consider getting a local SIM card at the airport or using an eSIM service like Saily (Referral code: ALBERT2447) before you travel. This will give you much more affordable data to navigate and stay in touch.

No Dar Papaya

    These are common-sense tips, and following them is simple!

  • Be Aware: Always be conscious of your surroundings, especially in crowded areas like markets, bus stations, or tourist spots.
  • Personal Belongings: Keep your bag zipped and in front of you. Don't hang it on the back of a chair in a restaurant.
  • Valuables: Keep valuables like phones, cameras, and passports out of sight when not in use. Avoid using your phone while walking on the street if possible.
  • Jewelry: Avoid wearing flashy or expensive-looking jewelry that might attract unwanted attention.
  • Taxi: Use reputable ride-sharing apps (Uber or Cabify) instead of hailing taxis on the street, especially at night.
  • Walking at Night: Stick to well-lit, busy streets. If you're unsure about an area, it's better to take a taxi.

    • You should not be scared but aware of the situation
