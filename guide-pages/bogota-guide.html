What to See

  • Monserrate: For a breathtaking view of the city, take the cable car or funicular up Monserrate mountain. It's best to go on a clear day!
  • La Candelaria: Explore the historic heart of Bogotá with its colorful colonial architecture, cobblestone streets, and vibrant street art.
  • Gold Museum (Museo del Oro): Discover one of the world's most dazzling collections of pre-Hispanic gold artifacts.
  • Botero Museum: Enjoy the works of Colombia's most famous artist, Fernando Botero, known for his "voluminous" figures.
  • Catedral de Sal: A incredible underground Catholic church built within the tunnels of a salt mine. A great day trip from Bogotá, located in the town of Zipaquirá.

Bogotá Dining & Nightlife Guide

We've sorted some ideas to help you get started, but don't limit yourself!

Restaurants & Fine Dining

  • Harry Sasson Fine dining at its best, with a mix of Colombian and international flavors crafted by one of the country's most renowned chefs.
  • Andrés Carne de Res A unique and festive restaurant offering a lively dining experience with colorful decor, creative dishes, and a party-like atmosphere that makes every visit memorable. Larger location in Chía (a short trip outside Bogotá).
  • Cumbia House Gaira Restaurant and live music venue co-founded by Carlos Vives, offering great food, cocktails, and a lively vibe.
  • El Chato Contemporary Colombian bistro blending modern techniques with traditional flavors, using fresh, local ingredients.
  • Cascajal Fine dining in Zona G, offering a tasting menu that reimagines Colombian flavors with French, Japanese, and Australian techniques.
  • Mesa Franca Creative reinterpretations of Colombian dishes in a warm, casual atmosphere, perfect for both everyday dining and special occasions.
  • Fiero Uruguayan-style steakhouse with premium cuts and a cozy atmosphere, perfect for meat lovers.
  • Antenna Japanese-style restaurant and music lounge with wood-fired dishes, cocktails, vinyl records, and live weekend music.
  • KO Asian Kitchen Modern Asian fusion with bold flavors and beautifully presented dishes.
  • Verano Contemporary restaurant with a relaxed vibe, serving creative dishes made with local ingredients.
  • Primi Cozy Italian restaurant in Bogotá offering homemade pasta, wood-fired pizzas, and an extensive wine list. Its charming indoor and outdoor seating makes it perfect for casual dining or special occasions.

Brunch & Others/Casual Dining

  • Crepes & Waffles Casual spot offering crepes, waffles, salads, and ice cream, perfect for brunch or any time of day.
  • MASA Bakery and café offering artisanal pastries, brunch dishes, and croissants in a cozy, minimalist setting.
  • Al Agua Patos Cozy eatery specializing in gourmet toasts with creative sweet and savory toppings. Perfect for brunch or casual meals with artisanal coffees and refreshing drinks.

Bars & Nightlife

  • Rooftop Vista Corona Panoramic city views, ideal for evening drinks with friends, watch out because at 6 p.m you'll be given a free mini corona.
  • El Mono Bandido Brewpub with multiple locations, serving craft beers, burgers, and eclectic decor in a lively atmosphere.
  • O.D.E.M. Rooftop bar in Chapinero with city views, offering tacos, drinks, and a mix of electronic, reggaeton, house, and salsa music.
  • Video Club Electronic music venue in Chapinero with an industrial vibe, two stages, and an outdoor terrace. Perfect for techno, house, and reggaeton fans.
  • Attic & Keller Stylish restaurant and beer garden with Colombian and international dishes, craft beers, and cocktails.

Cultural & Fun Activities

  • Tejo Turmequé Modern take on Colombia's traditional tejo, where players throw discs at explosive targets for a thrilling game. Enjoy drinks, music, and a lively social atmosphere.
  • Tejo La Embajada Another modern tejo venue with the same thrilling gameplay, drinks, music, and a social setting.

Markets & Food Experiences

  • Usaquén Flea Market Open-air market on weekends and holidays featuring handcrafted goods, street food, live music, and colonial architecture.
  • Paloquemao Market Lively and adventurous market where you can explore exotic fruits, fresh flowers, and traditional Colombian snacks.
  • Zona G (Gourmet) Neighborhood packed with top restaurants offering Colombian and international cuisines within walking distance.
